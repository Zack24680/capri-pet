## Personality translation

The pet combines self-assurance, teasing amusement, impatient reactions and curiosity. A cheerful expression is one state, rather than a claim that Capri is uniformly sweet. Her digital construction informed changing graphic expressions and flexible shapes rather than hard mechanical anatomy.

Earlier dialogue research consulted [episode-four](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/television/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e04-someone-that-i-used-to-know-transcript/) and [episode-seven](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/television/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/) transcript pages for register. Those sources were used as dialogue evidence, not as evidence of choreography or micro-timing. No transcript passages are redistributed here. Exact vocal delivery, exhaustive vocabulary, a never-blinking rule and quantitative claims about constituent minds are not part of this asset's specification.

The available Pets integration exposes visual assets and metadata, not a configurable dialogue or voice engine. Session-driven activity bubbles, where provided by a host, are a separate interface behavior. This download does not configure them.

## Artistic adaptation

The final direction is cute, rounded, fluid and weightless: a large expressive head, broad curved pink tufts, black flexible limbs, green face/patches and blue-white eye graphics. It deliberately avoids rigid armor, noisy texture, detached shards, loose motion effects and divided teeth.

Each standard state was generated independently. Left travel was generated separately so the character's asymmetry was preserved. The active-work row uses focused face/hand gestures and stays distinct from locomotion. Timing was synthesized to fit the required frame counts.

The gaze family keeps the body anchored, shifts the digital eye graphics within their black surrounds, and uses a small face/head flex. The internal graphic stack is an adaptation for readability; it should not be mistaken for a measured account of the original show's eye mechanics. Wide circles and simple white mouth glyphs remain identifiable at native size.
